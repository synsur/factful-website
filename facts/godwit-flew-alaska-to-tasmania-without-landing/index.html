A bird flew from Alaska to Tasmania without landing once.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Why it's true

B6 is a young godwit. In October 2022, it left Alaska. No food. No rest. Its organs shrank as it flew.

It flew past Vanuatu and New Caledonia. Didn't stop. And if a godwit lands on water, it's dead. It probably lost half its body weight.

11 days and one hour later: Tasmania. Godwits normally go to New Zealand. This one took a “wrong turn” and broke the world record.

The ritual

Tear it off. It's oddly satisfying.

Factful is a page-a-day calendar. Yesterday's page stays on top until you pull it away, with the sound of paper and a little tap you feel in your hand, and today's fact is waiting underneath.

Every page has a Why it's true: the story behind the fact and the source it came from, one tap away.

Every fact, checked

True is the minimum. Surprising is the bar.

Every page cites its source, and every number on it is matched against that source before it ships. Then comes the harder test: would a stranger stop scrolling for it? Most facts don't make it. We even retired 73 facts for being merely true.

History

Vikings were cutting trees in Canada in 1021, and a solar storm is how we know the exact year.

A burst of cosmic radiation in 993 left a radiocarbon spike in tree rings worldwide. Counting rings out from that spike in wood cut with metal tools at L'Anse aux Meadows, Newfoundland, showed all three trees were felled in 1021. It is the only secure date for Europeans in the Americas before Columbus.

Questions

Good questions.

Is Factful free?

Yes. One page a day, every day, with its source, your library and the widgets, free for good. Factful+ adds up to five pages a day, your choice of subjects and search.

Where do the facts come from?

From sources you can read yourself: encyclopedias, science agencies, museums, journals and major publications. Every page shows its source, with a link, under Why it's true.

How do you check them?

Every number in a fact is matched against the page it cites before it ships, and anything the source doesn't support is cut. If you ever spot a mistake, every page has a "Spot an error?" link that goes straight to us.

What data do you collect?

None. There's no account, no tracking and no analytics in the app. Your settings, history and saved pages stay on your device. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Read the privacy policy.

Will it nag me?

Never. One notification a day at the time you pick. No streaks, no guilt, no "we miss you", no promotions.

What if I don't open the app for a while?

Your pages keep coming. They're scheduled weeks ahead on your phone, so the notification arrives whether or not you open Factful.

How do I cancel Factful+?

In iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Factful. Cancelling keeps Factful+ until the end of the period you paid for. Restoring purchases on a new phone is one tap in Factful's Settings.

A man went 382 days without solid food.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Why it's true

A Scottish man went 382 days without solid food in 1965, and it was a medical record.

Angus Barbieri checked into a Dundee hospital for a short fast but insisted on continuing because he adapted so well. He lived on tea, coffee, sparkling water, vitamins and yeast extract, and fell from about 207 kg to about 82 kg.

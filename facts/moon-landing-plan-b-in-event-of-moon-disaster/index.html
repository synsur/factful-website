The Moon landing had a plan B. A funeral.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Why it's true

Nixon's speechwriter wrote this two days before.

IN EVENT OF MOON DISASTER

These brave men, Neil Armstrong and Edwin Aldrin, know that there is no hope for their recovery.

The plan: Nixon calls the widows-to-be. NASA ends communications. A clergyman commends their souls. The speech never had to be delivered.

Fate has ordained that the men who went to the moon to explore in peace will stay on the moon to rest in peace.

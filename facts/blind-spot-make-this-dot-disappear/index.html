Make this dot disappear.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Try it

  1. Close your left eye. Stare at the cross.
  2. Bring the phone slowly toward your face.

Why it's true

Gone? That's your blind spot. Your optic nerve exits the eye there. No light sensors. Your brain fills it in with what's around it.

Octopuses don't have one.

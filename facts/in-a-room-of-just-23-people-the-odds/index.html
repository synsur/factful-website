23 people. Even odds two birthdays match.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗YouTube ↗

Why it's true

In a room of just 23 people, the odds are about even that two of them share a birthday.

The trick is to count pairs, not people. Twenty-three people make 253 possible pairs, and each pair is its own chance at a match. Any one pair has a 99.73 percent chance of not matching, but multiply that across all 253 pairs and the chance of at least one shared birthday climbs to about 50 percent.

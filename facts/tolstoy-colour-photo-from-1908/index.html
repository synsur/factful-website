This colour photo of Tolstoy is from 1908.

Posted onInstagram ↗TikTok ↗

Why it's true

No AI. Prokudin-Gorsky shot him three times, in black and white. Once through red, once through green, once through blue. Stack the three, and you get this.

It ended up on postcards. That fame helped get him in front of the Tsar. The Tsar backed him to shoot Russia in colour. And threw in a darkroom on rails.

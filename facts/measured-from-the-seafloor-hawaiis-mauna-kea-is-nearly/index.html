This mountain is taller than Everest.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗YouTube ↗

Why it's true

Measured from the seafloor, Hawaii's Mauna Kea is nearly 33,500 feet tall, taller than Everest.

Only about 13,796 feet of Mauna Kea rises above sea level, and roughly 19,700 feet sits below it. Everest stands 29,029 feet above sea level, so Mauna Kea's total height beats it by thousands of feet.

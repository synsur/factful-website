Privacy

Nothing to collect.

Last updated 13 November 2026

The short version. Factful 11, the current app, collects no personal data. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. Your settings and the pages you've read stay on your device. Purchases go through Apple, and we never see your payment details. This page is about Factful 11; versions before it work differently, as Older versions of the app explains.

Who we are

Factful is made by Vickio Labs Inc. Questions about this policy go to help@factful.app.

What stays on your device

Factful keeps a few things so it can work: your delivery time, the subjects you've chosen, the pages you've received and saved, and your sound setting. They're stored on your iPhone or iPad, where Factful's own widget can read them too. They are not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Your daily page is scheduled by the app on your device, weeks in advance. There is no server sending them and no push token. You can turn notifications off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Factful+ is sold through Apple's App Store, which handles payment, billing and receipts under Apple's privacy policy. The app checks whether you have Factful+ on your device using Apple's StoreKit. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details.

Statistics from Apple

Apple gives every developer daily totals for their app, by country: downloads, free trials, purchases and renewals, cancellations, refunds and failed payments, and how many people have a subscription or a free trial. They never identify you. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may also give us anonymous statistics such as how many people keep using the app. You can turn that off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Older versions of the app

Versions of Factful before 11 still run on iPhones and iPads that haven't updated the app, including those that can't run iOS 17. They share information with outside services and with our own server.

  • Superwall runs the app's subscription screens, which are web pages loaded from Superwall's servers when the app opens, and those pages can load fonts from Google. Each time the app opens, Superwall receives the identifier iOS gives our apps on your device, an ID of its own for your install, your device model, iOS and app versions, language, region, currency, time zone, App Store country and network type, and whether dark mode and Low Power Mode are on. From your IP address, Superwall works out your approximate location, such as your city, region and country. It also receives the date you installed the app, whether you have Factful+, and events as you use the app, such as a subscription screen being shown. For a purchase, it receives the product, its price and Apple's transaction IDs. See Superwall's privacy policy.
  • Expo, if you allow notifications, receives your device's push token each time the app opens, with a random ID the app keeps on your device. Factful sends no push notifications through it. See Expo's privacy policy.
  • Our server, hosted by Supabase, receives your App Store receipt when the app checks for Factful+: after a purchase, and when you close a subscription screen or tap something that needs Factful+. It passes the receipt to Apple to confirm it and doesn't store it. Its logs note which products the receipt holds and when they expire. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Each of these services, Google included, sees your IP address, as any server you connect to does. Updating to Factful 11, or deleting the old app, stops all of this. What was already sent stays with each service, under its own policy.

Sources you open

Every fact links to its source. Tapping Read the source opens that website, which has its own privacy policy.

When you email us

If you write to help@factful.app, for example through "Spot an error?", we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply and to fix the fact, and don't keep them longer than that needs.

This website

factful.app uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and its fonts are served from this site. If you switch the pad's sound off, that choice is remembered in your own browser. Its App Store links name the kind of page they're on (for example ct=web-home), so Apple's App Store Connect can show us how many downloads came through this site, as a total, counting only people who chose to share analytics with app developers. If you arrived from ChatGPT, this browser tab remembers that so the links say so; nothing is sent to us. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Factful is suitable for all ages. Because Factful 11 collects no personal data, it collects none from children either. Older versions send the details above whatever the user's age.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will say what changed and when. The date at the top is the last time it was updated.

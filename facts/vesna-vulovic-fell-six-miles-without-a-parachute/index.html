She fell six miles. No parachute. She lived.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Why it's true

Flight attendant Vesna Vulovic fell about 10,160 metres without a parachute and survived.

She was the only survivor of JAT Flight 367, which crashed in Czechoslovakia in 1972 after an explosion. She spent days in a coma and was temporarily paralyzed from the waist down, but recovered almost fully. She remembered little of it and had no fear of flying afterward.

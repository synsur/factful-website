Jupiter has a moon bigger than Mercury.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Why it's true

Jupiter's moon Ganymede is larger than the planet Mercury.

Ganymede is the largest and most massive moon in the Solar System, and its internal ocean may hold more water than all of Earth's oceans combined. It is also the only moon known to generate its own magnetic field, and it orbits Jupiter in about seven days.

There are two skulls in this painting.

Posted onInstagram ↗TikTok ↗

Why it's true

An ambassador, a bishop, a table of expensive gadgets. And skull one: that grey slash on the floor. Holbein stretched it. On purpose.

Seen from the side, it's a skull.

Skull two is on the ambassador's hat. And top left, half hidden behind the curtain: Jesus.

It may have been made for a stairwell. Walk up, glance left: skull.

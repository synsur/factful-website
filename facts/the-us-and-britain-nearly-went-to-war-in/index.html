A dead pig nearly started a war.

Posted onYouTube ↗Instagram ↗TikTok ↗

Why it's true

The US and Britain nearly went to war in 1859 over a dead pig.

A farmer shot a British-owned pig rooting in his garden on San Juan Island, which both nations claimed. Troops massed on both sides. The only casualty of the 1859 crisis was the pig. The dispute was settled by arbitration, and the island became American in 1872.

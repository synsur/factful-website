Squeeze the universe into one year. How old are you?

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Why it's true

  1. The Big Bang: 13.8 billion years ago.

    That's January 1.

  2. The Sun: about 4.6 billion years ago.

    Around September 1. Fashionably late.

  3. Dinosaurs: maybe 243 million years ago.

    Around December 25. Merry Christmas.

  4. Humans: around 300,000 years ago.

    Around 11:48 pm on New Year's Eve.

  5. Farming: 11,700 years ago.

    11:59:33 pm.

Every second here is 438 years. So you? Not even one second old.

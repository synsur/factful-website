A day on Venus is longer than its year.

Posted onTikTok ↗Instagram ↗

Why it's true

Venus turns so slowly that one rotation takes about 243 Earth days, while its trip around the Sun takes about 225. It also spins backward compared with most planets, so on Venus the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.
